Hi Pete & Janis. Make yourself at home.

One sprite each, the house you share inside and out, a song, some lines, then one button. Everything you do is kept in this browser (not the tab: closing and coming back later is fine, as long as it is the same browser on the same computer and you pressed Save). Pack it up at the end.

1. Your characters

Pick a body, hair, clothes, glasses, hat. Type the name exactly as shown (Pete, Janis) and click Save to game. Save as often as you like; the last save under each name counts.

Open the character builder

2. The house

You start inside with the editor open on the right. Furniture: pick a kind from the dropdown, click a picture, click the floor (right click removes). Floor and Wallpaper recolour the room, and painting onto the black adds rooms (More room adds space). Front door: click where the way out should be. Walk around with WASD or the arrow keys to try it; red tiles are the ones you can't walk through. Press F7 (or Save) whenever you like what you see.

Open the house

Rugs and small things can be made walkable with X. The door doesn't lead anywhere here; that's on purpose.

Or start from a layout with a few rooms already there (this replaces the room and its furniture; you can still paint, grow and furnish it):

3. Outside

A patch of grass with your house in the middle. Your house lists other looks for the building: click one and the house changes. Trees, Plants & flowers and Garden things go anywhere on the grass (up to 8 tiles from the house; that's all the grass there is). Save with F7.

Open the outside

4. Music

What plays inside your house. Press play for a 10 second taste, or Play them all to hear every clip one after another, then tick the one you want.

5. Things you say

Add lines the character says when the farmer talks to them. Pick who, pick when, write the line, Add. As many as you like.
