Hi Pete & Janis. Make yourself at home.

One sprite each, the house you share, then one button. Paint floor onto the black to add rooms (More room adds space). Then some lines to say. Everything you do is kept in this browser until you pack it up, so use the same browser throughout.

1. Your characters

Pick a body, hair, clothes, glasses, hat. Type the name exactly as shown (Pete, Janis) and click Save to game. Save as often as you like; the last save under each name counts.

Open the character builder

2. The house

You start inside with the editor open on the right. Props places furniture (search the list, click a thumbnail, click the floor; right-click removes). Floor and Wallpaper change the room. Walk with WASD. Press F7 (or the Save button) whenever you like what you see.

Open the house

Rugs and small things can be made walkable with X. There is no door out; that's on purpose.

3. Things you say

Add lines the character says when the farmer talks to them. Pick who, pick when, write the line, Add. As many as you like.
